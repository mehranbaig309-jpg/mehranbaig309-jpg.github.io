No. 001 Writing & Chat

ChatGPT

A general-purpose AI chat assistant for drafting emails, brainstorming ideas, and answering questions on almost any subject. It can also generate images with DALL·E 3 built in, browse the web for current information, and run code directly in the chat. Widely used as an everyday starting point for both work and personal tasks.

No. 002 Writing & Chat

Claude

An AI assistant from Anthropic known for careful, well-structured writing and strong performance on long documents. It can read and discuss uploaded PDFs, spreadsheets, and images in the same conversation, and Projects let you group related chats and files so it keeps context across an ongoing task. A solid pick for research, editing, and reasoning through complex problems.

Free / from $20mo Visit ↗
No. 003 Writing & Chat

Gemini

Google's AI assistant, built directly into Search, Gmail, and Docs so it can draft, summarize, and answer questions without leaving apps you already use. It can also analyze images and, on paid tiers, generate them. A convenient option if your day-to-day work already runs through Google Workspace.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 004 Research

Perplexity

An AI-powered search engine that answers questions with citations to live web sources rather than generating prose purely from memory. Useful for fact-checking, current events, and research where you want to trace an answer back to where it came from. Includes a "Pro" mode for deeper, multi-source research.

Free / from $20mo Visit ↗
No. 005 Research

NotebookLM

Upload your own documents, notes, or slides and turn them into a searchable notebook you can ask questions against, with answers grounded in your source material. It can also generate summaries and even a podcast-style audio overview of your notes. Particularly useful for students and researchers working through a large stack of reading.

Free Visit ↗
No. 006 Research

Elicit

A research tool that helps you search, extract, and summarize findings across large numbers of academic papers at once. Instead of reading each abstract individually, you can ask a research question and get key findings pulled together automatically. Aimed at literature reviews and early-stage research.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 007 Image

Midjourney

An AI image generator, originally Discord-based but now also available via a web app, known for painterly, high-craft default aesthetics even from short prompts. Popular with artists and designers for concept art, illustrations, and stylized visuals. Generally considered one of the strongest options for image quality out of the box.

From $10mo Visit ↗
No. 008 Image

DALL·E 3

OpenAI's image generation model, built directly into ChatGPT, so you can generate and edit images in the same conversation as your text. It's particularly good at following detailed instructions and rendering readable text within images. A convenient option if you're already using ChatGPT for other tasks.

Included with ChatGPT Plus Visit ↗
No. 009 Image

Ideogram

An AI image generator with a particular strength most others struggle with: rendering clean, legible text inside graphics like posters, logos, and social media graphics. Useful when a design needs actual readable words baked into the image rather than gibberish text. Has a solid free tier for casual use.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 010 Image

Adobe Firefly

Adobe's generative image and design tool, built into Photoshop, Express, and other Creative Cloud apps for tasks like generative fill, background replacement, and text-to-image. Commercially safe licensing (trained on licensed and public domain content) makes it a common choice for professional and brand work. A natural fit if you already work inside Adobe's ecosystem.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 011 Video

Runway

A text-to-video and video-editing suite used widely for short-form generative film and experimental video work. Beyond generation, it includes AI editing tools like green-screen removal and motion tracking. Popular among filmmakers and creative studios exploring AI-assisted video production.

From $12mo Visit ↗
No. 012 Video

Pika

A fast text- and image-to-video generator aimed at quick, social-style clips rather than long cinematic output. The interface is built for rapid iteration, so you can generate several variations of a short clip in a few minutes. A good fit for social content creators who need speed over depth.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 013 Video

Sora

OpenAI's text-to-video model, capable of generating short, cinematic clips from a written prompt, including realistic motion and scene consistency. Access is currently bundled with ChatGPT Plus rather than sold separately. Best suited to short creative or concept clips rather than long-form video.

Included with ChatGPT Plus Visit ↗
No. 014 Video

CapCut

A video editing app with AI-assisted auto-captions, background removal, and smart cuts, widely used for short-form content aimed at TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. The free tier is generous enough for regular casual use. Popular with creators who want fast turnaround without a steep editing learning curve.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 015 Audio

ElevenLabs

A realistic text-to-speech and voice-cloning engine used for narration, audiobooks, and multilingual dubbing. Voices can sound convincingly natural across dozens of languages, and custom voice clones can be created from a short audio sample. A common choice for podcasters, video creators, and localization work.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 016 Audio

Suno

Generates full songs, including vocals and instrumentation, from a short text description of the style, mood, or lyrics you want. No musical training is needed to get a finished, listenable track in under a minute. Popular for quick jingles, demo tracks, and casual songwriting experiments.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 017 Audio

Udio

A text-to-music generator focused on studio-quality output across a wide range of genres, from full songs to instrumental beds. Tends to produce more polished-sounding mixes than some competitors, at the cost of a steeper learning curve for prompting. Aimed at both hobbyists and more serious independent musicians.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 018 Audio

Descript

Edits audio and video by editing the automatically-generated text transcript — delete a word in the transcript and it cuts the matching audio/video. Also includes AI voice cleanup, filler-word removal, and an "Overdub" feature to fix a flubbed line without re-recording. A strong pick for podcasters and video editors who prefer editing text over a timeline.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 019 Code

GitHub Copilot

An in-editor AI pair programmer that suggests and completes code as you type, trained on a huge volume of public code. Works inside popular editors like VS Code and JetBrains IDEs, and can also answer coding questions in a chat panel. One of the most widely adopted AI coding tools, especially in professional teams.

From $10mo Visit ↗
No. 020 Code

Cursor

An AI-native code editor built around chat-driven edits that can span multiple files across an entire codebase, not just the one you have open. It can plan a change, apply it across the relevant files, and explain what it did. Popular with developers who want an AI that understands full project context, not just the current file.

Free / from $20mo Visit ↗
No. 021 Code

Claude Code

A command-line AI agent from Anthropic that plans, writes, and runs code changes directly in your terminal, across an entire project. It can read your codebase, make multi-file edits, run tests, and iterate based on the results, all from natural-language instructions. Aimed at developers comfortable working from the command line rather than a GUI.

Usage-based Visit ↗
No. 022 Code

Replit

A browser-based coding environment with an AI agent that can scaffold a new app, write the code, and deploy it, all without local setup. Useful for prototyping ideas quickly or for beginners who don't want to configure a development environment first. Runs entirely in the browser, so no installation is required.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 023 Productivity

Notion AI

A writing and summarizing assistant built directly into Notion's docs and databases, so it can draft content, summarize a page, or fill in database fields without switching tools. Particularly useful for turning messy meeting notes into structured, actionable summaries. Works best if your team already organizes work inside Notion.

No. 024 Productivity

Otter.ai

Transcribes and summarizes meetings in real time, producing a searchable transcript and automatic summary once the call ends. Integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams to join and record calls automatically. A practical way to stop relying on rushed manual note-taking during meetings.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 025 Productivity

Superhuman

A premium email client with AI-drafted replies, keyboard-shortcut-driven navigation, and features aimed squarely at fast inbox triage. Designed for people who process a very high volume of email daily and want to cut the time spent per message. The paid-only pricing reflects its positioning toward professionals and executives.

From $25mo Visit ↗
No. 026 Productivity

Motion

Automatically schedules your tasks and meetings into your calendar based on priority and deadlines, and re-schedules things when your day changes. Aims to replace manual calendar management and separate to-do lists with one automated system. Best suited to people juggling a high volume of shifting daily commitments.

From $19mo Visit ↗
No. 027 Design & Slides

Gamma

Generates full presentation decks and documents from a short outline or prompt, handling layout and design automatically as you add content. Slides can be edited afterward like a normal deck, and the tool also supports embedding interactive content like polls. A fast way to get from a rough outline to a presentable deck.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 028 Design & Slides

Canva Magic Studio

A suite of AI design tools built into Canva, covering background generation, text-to-image, one-click resizing across formats, and automatic background removal. Since it's part of Canva's existing free tier, it's accessible to people with no design background. A practical option for social graphics, presentations, and marketing materials.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 029 Design & Slides

Framer AI

Generates and edits full marketing websites from a text prompt, including layout, copy placeholders, and responsive design, ready to publish without writing code. Existing sites can also be redesigned or restyled through follow-up prompts. Aimed at small businesses and marketers who need a site live quickly.

Free / paid tiers Visit ↗
No. 030 Design & Slides

Galileo AI

Turns short text prompts into editable UI screen designs, generating layouts, components, and styling that a product or design team can then refine. Intended to speed up the earliest stage of product design, before a designer commits time to a polished mockup. A niche but useful tool for product teams moving fast from idea to prototype.

Generates presenter-style videos from text using realistic AI avatars, so you can produce narrated video content without a camera, microphone, or on-camera talent. Supports dozens of languages and avatar styles, making it useful for training videos, product explainers, and localized content at scale. A practical option for teams that need frequent video content but limited production resources.
