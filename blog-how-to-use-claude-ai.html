How to Use Claude AI: A Step-by-Step Guide for Beginners

Claude is one of the most capable AI assistants available today, built by Anthropic — but the interface doesn't explain itself. This guide walks through everything from creating an account to writing prompts that actually get you good answers, one step at a time.

What is Claude AI?

Claude is an AI chat assistant made by Anthropic. You can ask it questions, have it draft or edit writing, explain code, analyze a document you upload, or just think through a problem out loud with it. It runs in a browser at claude.ai, with mobile and desktop apps available too — no installation required to get started.

STEP 1

Create your free account

  • Go to claude.ai in your browser.
  • Click "Sign up" and continue with an email address or a Google account.
  • If you sign up with email, you'll get a one-time code to verify — enter it and you're in.
  • No credit card is needed for the free plan.
STEP 2

Start your first conversation

  • Once logged in, you'll see a message box in the middle of the screen.
  • Type anything — a question, a task, or something you want explained — and press Enter.
  • Claude replies in the same window, and you can keep replying to go back and forth, the same way you'd text someone.
  • Each new topic is best started as a fresh conversation, using the "New chat" button, so old context doesn't confuse the answer.
STEP 3

Upload a document or image

  • Click the paperclip / attachment icon next to the message box.
  • Choose a PDF, Word document, image, or spreadsheet from your device.
  • Ask a question about it directly — e.g. "Summarize this" or "What's the main argument on page 3?"
  • This is useful for long reports, contracts, lecture notes, or screenshots you want explained.
STEP 4

Write a prompt that gets a good answer

The quality of the answer usually comes down to how the question is asked. A few habits that consistently help:

  • Say what you want, specifically. "Write a 200-word product description for a leather backpack, casual tone" beats "write something about my backpack."
  • Give context. Who it's for, what it's used for, and any constraints (length, tone, audience) all shape the answer.
  • Ask for a format. "As a bulleted list", "in a table", or "as three short paragraphs" all change how the answer comes back.
  • Iterate. If the first answer isn't quite right, say what to change rather than starting over — Claude keeps the conversation's context.
STEP 5

Use Projects for ongoing work

  • If you're working on something over several days (a research paper, a business plan, a coding project), create a Project from the sidebar.
  • Add relevant files and notes to the Project once — every chat inside it can then reference that material automatically.
  • This saves you from re-uploading or re-explaining the same background every time you open a new chat.
STEP 6

Choose free or paid

  • The free plan gives a set number of messages every few hours — enough for casual, everyday use.
  • Paid plans raise those limits and unlock more capable models for heavier or more frequent use.
  • A sensible approach: start free, and only upgrade once you're actually hitting the usage limit regularly.
Quick tip: Claude tends to do especially well with long documents and careful, detailed writing — if that's your main use case, it's worth trying alongside whatever assistant you already use.

Frequently asked questions

Is Claude AI free to use?

Yes. There's a free plan with a message limit that resets periodically, alongside paid plans for heavier use.

Is Claude better than ChatGPT?

Neither is universally "better" — they're strong at different things. Many people use both. See our ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini comparison for a fuller breakdown.

Can Claude read files I upload?

Yes — PDFs, images, Word documents, and spreadsheets can all be uploaded and discussed directly in a conversation.

Do I need to download an app to use Claude?

No, it works fully in a web browser at claude.ai. Desktop and mobile apps exist too, if you prefer them.
