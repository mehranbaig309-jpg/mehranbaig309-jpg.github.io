ChatGPT Kya Hai Aur Kaise Use Karein? Complete Beginner's Guide

Published 2026 · AI Stackroom · Tool Guide

Agar aap ne AI ke baare mein sirf ek naam suna hai, to wo shayad ChatGPT hi hoga. Ye OpenAI ka banaya hua ek chat-based AI assistant hai jo likhne, samajhne, aur sawalon ke jawab dene mein madad karta hai — bilkul jaise kisi ache guide se baat kar rahe hon.

Quick Info:
Price: Free / Paid plans start from $20/month
Website: chat.openai.com

ChatGPT Kya Hai?

ChatGPT ek "large language model" hai — yani ek aisa AI system jo insani zaban samajhta hai aur usi tarah jawab deta hai. Aap isse kuch bhi pooch sakte hain: emails likhwana, essay banana, coding mein madad, ya sirf kisi topic par tafseel se baat karna.

ChatGPT Kaise Use Karein — Step by Step

  1. Account banayein: chat.openai.com par jaein aur apna email ya Google account se sign up karein.
  2. Chat shuru karein: Neeche wale box mein apna sawal ya request likhein — jaise "Mujhe ek business email likh do".
  3. Jawab check karein: ChatGPT foran jawab dega. Agar behtar chahiye to "isay aur professional bana do" jaisi follow-up request bhi kar sakte hain.
  4. Conversation continue karein: Har naya sawal pichli baat ka context yaad rakhta hai, is liye aap silsila continue kar sakte hain.

ChatGPT Ke Faide

  • Waqt bachaata hai: Emails, reports, ya social media captions minutes mein taiyar ho jaate hain.
  • Sikhne mein madadgar: Koi bhi topic samjhaane ke liye simple zaban mein explain kar deta hai.
  • Coding assistant: Programmers ke liye code likhna, bugs dhoondhna, aur samjhaana asaan bana deta hai.
  • Brainstorming partner: Naye ideas, business plans, ya content ideas nikalne mein madad karta hai.
  • 24/7 available: Kisi bhi waqt, bina wait kiye jawab milta hai.

Kis Ke Liye Faydemand Hai?

Students apne assignments samajhne ke liye, business owners emails aur marketing content ke liye, developers coding madad ke liye, aur content creators ideas aur drafts ke liye ChatGPT ko roz istemal karte hain.

Ek Chota Sa Tip

Behtar jawab paane ke liye apna sawal jitna clear aur specific likhenge, jawab utna hi accurate aayega. Misal ke tor par "mujhe email likh do" ki jagah "mujhe apne boss ko leave ki application likh do, tone formal ho" likhna zyada acha result dega.
