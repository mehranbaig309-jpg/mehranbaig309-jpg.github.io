What Is ChatGPT and How to Use It? Complete Beginner's Guide

Published 2026 · AI Stackroom · Tool Guide

If you've heard just one name in the world of AI, it's probably ChatGPT. Built by OpenAI, it's a chat-based AI assistant that helps with writing, understanding complex topics, and answering questions — much like having a conversation with a knowledgeable guide available any time of day.

Quick Info:
Price: Free / Paid plans start from $20/month
Website: chat.openai.com

What Is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a "large language model" — an AI system trained to understand and generate human-like text. You can ask it almost anything: draft an email, write an essay, help debug code, or simply have an in-depth conversation about a topic you're curious about.

How to Use ChatGPT — Step by Step

  1. Create an account: Go to chat.openai.com and sign up using your email or a Google account.
  2. Start chatting: Type your question or request into the message box — for example, "Write a professional email requesting a meeting."
  3. Review the response: ChatGPT replies instantly. If you want it refined, simply ask a follow-up like "make this more formal" or "shorten it."
  4. Continue the conversation: Each new message remembers the context of the conversation, so you can keep building on your original request.

Benefits of ChatGPT

  • Saves time: Emails, reports, and social media captions can be drafted in minutes.
  • Helps you learn: Explains complex topics in simple, easy-to-understand language.
  • Coding assistant: Helps developers write code, find bugs, and understand unfamiliar codebases.
  • Brainstorming partner: Generates fresh ideas for content, business plans, or creative projects.
  • Available 24/7: No waiting — get answers any time, day or night.

Who Benefits Most From ChatGPT?

Students use it to understand assignments, business owners rely on it for emails and marketing copy, developers use it for coding help, and content creators use it daily for ideas and first drafts.

A Quick Tip

The more specific your prompt, the better the response. Instead of asking "write an email," try "write a formal leave application email to my manager" — the clearer the request, the more accurate and useful the output.
